DRONA JEE class 11th  Batch Details 

1.⁠ ⁠We'll cover class 11th  Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics for JEE aspirants preparing for JEE Main & JEE Advanced 2028.

2.⁠ ⁠All lectures will be conducted Live Online, and in case you miss a session, recordings will be available to students on the Mission JEET App/Website.

3.⁠ ⁠Classes will be scheduled in a structured timetable suitable for consistent preparation.

4.⁠ ⁠There will be two classes per day, and classes will be conducted 6 days a week.

5.⁠ ⁠Each class will be accompanied by PDF Notes, which will be uploaded after the lecture on the Mission JEET Application.

6.⁠ ⁠Daily Practice Problems (DPPs) along with Video Solutions by the Faculty will be provided to strengthen concept clarity and problem-solving skills.

7.⁠ ⁠Chapterwise Practice Sheets / Modules (JEE Main + Advanced Level) pdfswill be provided for comprehensive practice.

8.⁠ ⁠A summary lecture of each chapter will be provided after chapter completion for quick revision and concept reinforcement.

9.⁠ ⁠Biweekly scheduled tests will be conducted according to the test planner to track your preparation progress.

10.⁠ ⁠Specialized JEE Advanced Level Tests will also be conducted to evaluate your preparation at the Advanced level.

11.⁠ ⁠To support your Board preparation along with JEE, free access to the Prarambh Science Batch will be provided.

12.⁠ ⁠After completion of the syllabus, dedicated revision classes will be conducted to strengthen concepts and improve exam readiness.

13.⁠ ⁠Classes will commence on 6th April 2026, and the complete syllabus will be covered by 31st January 2027.

14.⁠ ⁠The revision phase will continue until 28th February 2027, followed by tests until 15th March 2027.

15.⁠ ⁠The entire course will remain accessible to you until the JEE 2028 examinations.

16.⁠ ⁠This batch is non-refundable and non-transferable.
