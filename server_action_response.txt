DRONA JEE class 11th &nbsp;Batch Details&nbsp;</strong></h1>

<p>1.⁠ ⁠We&#39;ll cover class 11th &nbsp;<strong>Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics</strong> for JEE aspirants preparing for <strong>JEE Main &amp; JEE Advanced 2028</strong>.</p>

<p>2.⁠ ⁠All lectures will be conducted <strong>Live Online</strong>, and in case you miss a session, <strong>recordings will be available to students on the Mission JEET App/Website</strong>.</p>

<p>3.⁠ ⁠Classes will be scheduled in a <strong>structured timetable suitable for consistent preparation</strong>.</p>

<p>4.⁠ ⁠There will be <strong>two classes per day</strong>, and classes will be conducted <strong>6 days a week</strong>.</p>

<p>5.⁠ ⁠Each class will be accompanied by <strong>PDF Notes</strong>, which will be uploaded after the lecture on the <strong>Mission JEET Application</strong>.</p>

<p>6.⁠ ⁠<strong>Daily Practice Problems (DPPs)</strong> along with <strong>Video Solutions by the Faculty</strong> will be provided to strengthen concept clarity and problem-solving skills.</p>

<p>7.⁠ ⁠<strong>Chapterwise Practice Sheets / Modules (JEE Main + Advanced Level)</strong> pdfswill be provided for comprehensive practice.</p>

<p>8.⁠ ⁠A <strong>summary lecture of each chapter</strong> will be provided after chapter completion for <strong>quick revision and concept reinforcement</strong>.</p>

<p>9.⁠ ⁠<strong>Biweekly scheduled tests</strong> will be conducted according to the <strong>test planner</strong> to track your preparation progress.</p>

<p>10.⁠ ⁠Specialized <strong>JEE Advanced Level Tests</strong> will also be conducted to evaluate your preparation at the <strong>Advanced level</strong>.</p>

<p>11.⁠ ⁠To support your <strong>Board preparation along with JEE</strong>, <strong>free access to the Prarambh Science Batch</strong> will be provided.</p>

<p>12.⁠ ⁠After completion of the syllabus, <strong>dedicated revision classes</strong> will be conducted to strengthen concepts and improve exam readiness.</p>

<p>13.⁠ ⁠Classes will <strong>commence on 6th April 2026</strong>, and the <strong>complete syllabus will be covered by 31st January 2027</strong>.</p>

<p>14.⁠ ⁠The <strong>revision phase will continue until 28th February 2027</strong>, followed by <strong>tests until 15th March 2027</strong>.</p>

<p>15.⁠ ⁠The <strong>entire course will remain accessible to you until the JEE 2028 examinations</strong>.</p>

<p>16.⁠ ⁠This batch is <strong>non-refundable and non-transferable</strong>.</p>
